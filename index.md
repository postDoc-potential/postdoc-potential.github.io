<h1>PostDoc Potential</h1>
<p align="justify"> A platform created for and by early-career researchers navigating postdoctoral research</p>
<h2>Our Story </h2>
<p align="justify"> <b>PostDoc Potential</b> started as a dinner table discussion among a group of fresh PhDs in 2025. <i>What if we create a platform for people like us, transitioning from PhD to postDoc</i>. A platform where postDoctoral fellows can share their insights into project proposal writing, looking for suitable fellowships, and career planning.</p>
