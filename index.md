_Next Step after your PhD_

## Our Story

PostDoc Potential started as a dinner table discussion among a group of fresh PhDs in 2025. What if we create a platform for people like us, who are also transitioning from PhD to postDoct. A platform where postDoctoral fellows can share their insights into project proposal writing, looking for suitable fellowships, and career planning.
