## Privacy & data handling
{: #privacy}

If you fill out the survey on this site, here's what happens to it:

- **Responses are treated as anonymous** — the collected responses are not
  linked to individual participants or used to identify respondents.
- **Storage** — responses are stored via Google Sheets/Google Apps Script,
  which functions as a data processor with technical access to what's
  submitted.
- **No public sharing or sale** — data isn't shared publicly or sold to
  third parties. Note that Google, as the storage provider, isn't the
  same as "no external party has access"; this section will be updated
  if the backend changes.
