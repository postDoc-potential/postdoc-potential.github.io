# Privacy & Data Handling

<br>

## Earth Sciences Career Workshop Survey

The Earth Sciences Career Workshop Survey is organised by the PostDoc Potential and Climate Dialogues Way Forward (CDWF) team to gather perspectives from early-career researchers in earth sciences and related fields regarding postdoctoral opportunities and workshop design.

### Information Collected

This survey does not collect personally identifiable information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Institutional identification details
* Any other direct personal identifiers

Survey responses are collected only for the purpose of understanding participant perspectives and improving the design of the career workshop.

### Anonymous Responses

All survey responses are treated as anonymous. The collected responses are not linked to individual participants or used to identify respondents.

### Data Storage and Access

Survey responses are stored securely using Google Sheets and related Google Apps Script workflows.

Access to the collected responses is limited to authorised members of:

* PostDoc Potential
* Climate Dialogues Way Forward (CDWF)

The collected data will not be shared publicly or provided to external parties.

### Use of Responses

Survey responses will be used solely for educational and research-related purposes, including:

* Planning and improving the Earth Sciences Career Workshop
* Understanding the needs and perspectives of early-career researchers
* Developing workshop components and activities

### Data Retention

Survey responses will be retained only for as long as necessary for workshop planning, analysis, and related educational purposes.
